Retirement Corpus Guide

What this calculator does

This tool projects your living expenses at retirement after inflation, estimates the corpus required to support those expenses, and suggests a monthly investment amount to aim for that target.

How to use it

Enter your monthly expense today, years left until retirement, expected retirement duration, inflation, and assumed returns before and after retirement. The calculator then translates those assumptions into a planning estimate.

Example usage

If you spend Rs.60,000 per month today and plan to retire in 20 years, inflation can materially raise your future expense requirement. This calculator helps connect that future lifestyle cost to a present-day savings target.
