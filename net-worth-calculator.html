Why Track Net Worth?

Net worth gives you a cleaner financial health metric than income alone because it captures both assets and debt in one number. It can improve even when salary stays flat if debt shrinks or investments grow.

This calculator is meant for quick snapshots. For serious tracking, reuse the same categories monthly or quarterly so the trend line becomes more meaningful than any single reading.
