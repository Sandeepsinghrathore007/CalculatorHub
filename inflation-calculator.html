How to Read the Result

The projected future price shows what the same item or expense may cost after compounding the chosen inflation rate over the selected number of years.

The buying-power figure shows what the same nominal amount may effectively feel like in future real terms. If inflation is positive, that number is lower than today’s starting amount.
