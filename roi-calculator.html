How It Works

ROI is calculated as net profit divided by total amount invested. Net profit is the current or final value plus any cash received, minus the original investment.

Annualized return is shown only when you provide a holding period greater than zero. That figure helps compare investments held for different lengths of time on a like-for-like yearly basis.
